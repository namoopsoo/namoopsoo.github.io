## Statins and GLP-1
Dr Nick's video<sup>[1](#references)</sup>, on GLP-1 lowering effects of atorvastatins. Interesting I was reading AI Snake Oil<sup>[4](#references)</sup>, but one might broaden modeling to non-ML snake oil too?

Of course need to acknowledge that  as Nick says in the video, the take away is not that all statins are all bad forever. It's instead, let's better understand who might benefit from statins because they would still get a benefit while accepting the risk of the GLP-1  reduction. It's all , risk benefit analysis.

Nick makes this other intriguing point, that a lot of medicine is broad population medicine, because that is more economically cost-effective, but he is suggesting that maybe individualized medicine, especially for drugs as a better alternative for outcomes.

## Dave Feldman on LDL CVD model
Joseph Everett citing<sup>[2](#references)</sup> Dave Feldman, on how the model used by modern mediccine, associating LDL with cardiovascular disease,--*similar to many of the flaws pointed out by the authors of AI Snake oil*<sup>[3](#references)</sup>--may be based on a non-representative sample and therefore not generalizable. He says the population of people used  skewed unhealthy and perhaps the more generalizing covariates are high HDL and low triglycerides.
