Maybe "old fashioned " is still just "how the world works", so cheapest  bid always wins , so I wonder what is "the optimal strategy "?

Every contract will always be renegotiated w.r.t. the iron cross, that is, more time or less scope or lower quality. (Unless of course you already built the solution and no time required for updates)


So which is better : 
(A) spend a long time to precisely lay out Level of Effort and Statement of Work and eat anything you missed and hold customer accountable to anything they missed .

Or 
(B) guesstimate Level of Effort , and loose Statement of Work , but double or triple it simply. So you will probably win way fewer contracts but you'll waste less time on pointless estimation.
