##  Gantt Chart Fever
I didn't realize Henry Gantt was Taylor's associate!? Taylor and Gantt were all about collecting a lot of data and optimizing manufacturing. At one point this started getting applied to novel stuff? 

can we sort of historically pinpoint the rise of the MBA managerial model of the topdown waterfall gantt chart? This is the idea that some smart person or team, maybe a manager or  architect will preplan a greenfield project, and all of its steps, and then they will scotch tape those steps together in a project timeline, attach a budget to it and throw it over a wall to Individual contributors to implement this . and perhaps in lots of industries or project types where there are enough past projects offering past data to hedge against the Planning Fallacy,  experienced people can likely craft something reasonable with reasonable buffer (well side note they might not win the contract unless they loosen the budget beyond what they calculate on the back of their envelopes). However the software industry (and   others too as observed by Daniel Kahneman ) famously reacted to this with the Agile Manifesto arguing that most so called experts cannot really get waterfall working on average, or at least that they cannot tell the difference between "this new project  is sufficiently similar to all of these  historical projects so we can tighten our confidence intervals",    vs, "lets ignore statistics and decide we know better".
