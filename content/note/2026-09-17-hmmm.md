Thinking through the problem that software platform teams have lots of forces pulling at them making dynamics interesting. I have also been watching an astronomy series on H.B.O. Lately, so i have some space analogies on my brain now too that are making me giddy. Some of the forces are external, including, users reporting bugs, users experiencing friction , users asking questions , failures occurring in prod from bugs, from  data issues, from cloud outages, from runtime package issues, devops  team in the company making unexpected changes affecting deployments and or infra, cloud infra getting deprecated, company cost cutting asking for cloud consolidation, company adopting SAFe poking the team about using various predictability metrics. And there are of course lots of internal forces too, --and you night call them drives too--, like, the team drive to reduce the friction and bugs experienced by platform users, but also the drive to reduce their own internal friction  experienced when trying to do anything. That might be the drive to improve the platform in ways users perceive directly or to reduce techt debt which   users perceive  indirectly , by wqy of smoother use of the platform.
