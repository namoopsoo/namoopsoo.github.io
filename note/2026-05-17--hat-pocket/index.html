Note on creating this image#

Original prompt to Chat GPT#

Realistic Image of someone in shorts pulling a pocket out, inverting it, revealing a small hole the pocket lining has. They are of the facial expression sort of like “hide the pain harry”. They are holding a slightly screen cracked telephone in their other hand. This image has a second vertical pane as well, on the right. This pane is further split into top and bottom, the same person shows they are putting now their phone into a thin hat, and their mood is like neutralish. Now the last bottom pane here, we see them half way, putting their thin hat, that visibly has the geometry of a phone rectangle , into their pocket. Now their facial expression is “clever” . And there is also a thin frame at the top where a title reads “Hat Pocket” The panes, arranged, are like this ,

Updates#

This feels a bit wasteful, but in the original image, the pocket-hole was just not large enough. I prompted two more times about enlarging its size. The third time it was perfect!

Why the title#

At the risk of explaining the joke, this is a nod to hot pockets1.
