withings wireless scale infinite loop

holding pattern#

Kind of out of the blue, my Withings Body+ screen is on but theres nothing there. After some reseting, also cannot connect by phone. Removed it from the Withings app on my phone, trying to reconnect the scale as a new scale, but searching for the device, I see the Body+ c0 pop up, I select it, but then I am right back to the search for device screen.

It is an infinite loop.

a different phone?#

At one point I thought it was working, trying someone elses phone, but ultimately, still failed.

Ah the troubleshooting instructions helped!#

The support page had the important insight , to Forget the Body+ in the bluetooth settings ! And then repeating the steps, everything worked,

except that at the end 😅 after everything, the scale said the battery was dead haha. Even though I did try before, replacing the batteries , looks like the important combination was, both replacing the batteries and forgetting the bluetooth device .

Still though, the Withings app could use some work#

It would have been nice not to experience the odd infinite loop of selecting the detected device and being asked to detect a device again, over and over again. Instead, it would have been nice to just get a message explaining that perhaps I should forget the device from my bluetooth or replace my devices batteries since my app did ultimately show me the scale’s battery level was 0% haha.
